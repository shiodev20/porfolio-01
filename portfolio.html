2013

Customer Experience

Understanding people in context

  • Context

    Customer issues rarely exist in isolation. Understanding a complaint required looking across the customer journey, operational touchpoints, expectations, available information and the context surrounding each situation.

    Key Contribution

    I investigated complex customer feedback, handled escalations and crisis situations, audited service quality across customer touchpoints, supported training planning and workshops, and collaborated with IT on customer-service systems including CRM, touchpoint evaluation and Lost & Found.

    Achievement / Milestone

    Contributed to the development of customer-service systems with IT while combining case investigation, service-quality auditing and complex feedback resolution across multiple customer-journey touchpoints.

    What It Built in Me

    Problem diagnosisContext awarenessConnecting the dotsCustomer perspectiveSystems thinking

    Looking back, this is one of the earliest professional environments in which my Analytical identity became visible: before solving a problem, I learned to gather information across multiple touchpoints, understand context and question what was actually happening.

2016

Facilitation & Training

Helping people learn

  • Context

    In a fast-moving airline environment, learning had to work across frontline employees, team leaders and supervisors while responding continuously to changing operational and customer-service needs.

    Key Contribution

    I owned the learning cycle from needs analysis and content revision through facilitation and post-training evaluation. I led core curricula in Customer Service Excellence, Effective Communication and Teamwork & Collaboration across ILT, virtual ILT, e-learning/LMS and blended learning formats.

    Achievement / Milestone

    Expanded learning across four delivery approaches—classroom, virtual classroom, LMS/e-learning and blended learning—while supporting development across three workforce levels: employees, team leaders and supervisors.

    What It Built in Me

    FacilitationInstructional designAdult learningBlended learningLearning evaluation

    This was the chapter where “knowing something” and “helping another person learn it” became clearly different skills for me. It built the foundation for my Teaching/Coaching identity and later Learning Design work.

2021

Organizational L&D

Designing capability systems

  • Context

    The role required learning to respond directly to organizational needs rather than operate as a separate training function—connecting business priorities, capability gaps, curriculum, learning investment and employee development.

    Key Contribution

    I managed learning projects from Training Needs Analysis through strategy, annual curricula, budgeting, delivery and evaluation; redesigned and facilitated orientation for management and non-management employees; supported e-learning systems; and established an internal Facilitator Pool.

    Achievement / Milestone

    Established an internal Facilitator Pool to strengthen in-house learning capability and reduce dependence on external delivery, while expanding employee access to learning through e-learning systems and cross-functional HR initiatives.

    What It Built in Me

    Learning strategyCapability developmentBusiness partneringInternal knowledge transferLearning ecosystem thinking

    This chapter sharpened an idea that remains important in my work today: good learning should build capability inside the learner or system, rather than creating long-term dependency on the person delivering it.

2023

Experiential Learning

Designing conditions for reflection

  • Context

    Many development experiences rely heavily on verbal instruction and cognitive understanding. Thom Thao explored another question: what happens when reflection and personal development are approached through multi-sensory, creative experiences?

    Key Contribution

    I founded and designed a portfolio of experiences using art, sound and aroma, and facilitated workshops for individuals and corporate groups around personal growth, connection, reflection and creative engagement.

    Achievement / Milestone

    Built a multi-sensory learning and wellbeing portfolio across three primary formats—art, sound and aroma-based experiences—and extended these formats into corporate workshops designed to support team connection, engagement and creative energy.

    Brand collaborations — coming soon

    What It Built in Me

    Experiential learningCreative facilitationLearning conditionsReflection beyond instruction

    This chapter expanded my understanding that meaningful learning does not happen only through information. Environment, experience, emotion and the way a person is invited to reflect can all shape what they are able to see and learn.

2024

Education Leadership

Developing the whole learner

  • Context

    Leading an extracurricular education center for children aged 3–14 required moving beyond the delivery of individual subjects toward a broader question: how can learning develop cognitive ability while also supporting emotional and creative development?

    Key Contribution

    I lead strategic operations, curriculum development, educator and learner development, partnerships and educational initiatives. A central curriculum direction has been integrating Soroban mental mathematics with emotional intelligence and creative thinking rather than treating cognitive performance as the sole learning outcome.

    Achievement / Milestone

    International achievement

    Led teams and learners to 1st, 2nd and 3rd prize levels across the 2025 Genting International Abacus and Mental Arithmetic Competition, Malaysia; 2024 PAMA - Pacific Abacus and Mental Arithmetic Association, Vietnam.

    National learning platform

    TiTBrain was designated by S.A.M.A as the sole authorized unit to host Vietnam's 2025 Global Online Soroban Competition.

    Curriculum innovation

    Developed curricula combining Soroban mental mathematics, emotional intelligence and creative thinking.

    Education voice

    Featured in national media in connection with Positive Education, Constructivist pedagogy and English integration in education.

    Press links — coming soon

    What It Built in Me

    Education leadershipChild developmentCurriculum architecturePositive EducationConstructivismDiversity Learning Design

    This chapter moved my thinking from “teaching content” toward developing the whole learner. It also deepened the question that now connects to Family Education: what conditions allow a child to develop not only ability, but also confidence, emotional capacity and independent thinking?

2025+

Digital Education

Personalization & technology-enabled learning

  • Context

    Education is increasingly moving toward digital and personalized learning. The work called for educators who could translate pedagogical thinking into learning materials suitable for digital environments and contribute within a multidisciplinary educator community.

    Key Contribution

    I collaborate with a community of experts to develop and refine digital learning materials, bringing pedagogical perspectives and creative teaching approaches into the development process.

    Achievement / Milestone

    Selected to participate as an Educreator in an initiative focused on building a community of creative educators and supporting digital transformation in education, with the broader aim of making personalized learning more accessible to Vietnamese learners.

    Explore more

    What It Built in Me

    Digital learningPersonalizationCross-disciplinary collaborationTechnology-enabled education

    This chapter is strengthening my ability to think beyond the classroom and explore how technology can extend, personalize and scale learning while keeping the learner at the center.
